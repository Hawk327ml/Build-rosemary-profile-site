Know your herb

About Rosemary

Rosemary is a fragrant herb with needle-like leaves. It grows best in sunny places with well-drained soil. It can be used for cooking, tea, dried herbs, and natural home fragrance.

Sunlight

Give it at least 6 hours of bright, direct sun each day.

Soil

Use loose, well-drained potting mix in a pot with drainage holes.

Water

Feel the topsoil first. Water deeply only when it is dry.

Uses

Enjoy small sprigs in meals, tea, dried blends, or home fragrance.

Notice, understand, fix

Common Rosemary Problems & Solutions

Tap a question for a simple first action. Plant problems often come from water, light, or airflow.

Beginner tip

Change one care habit at a time, then watch the plant for several days.

Yellow leaves often mean the roots are staying too wet. Pause watering, let the topsoil dry, and make sure excess water can leave the pot.

Check whether the soil is completely dry and whether the pot is in harsh heat or strong wind. Water slowly if dry, but do not leave the pot standing in water.

Slow growth is commonly linked to low light or cool, damp soil. Move the plant to a brighter spot with good airflow and avoid frequent watering.

Use a pot with open drainage holes, choose loose soil, empty the saucer after watering, and always test the topsoil before adding more water.

Move the plant away from other pots, remove visible pests gently, and rinse affected areas with water. Check the plant again over the next few days.
